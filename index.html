Our Cats

Our cats are cute, loving and very special. That is exactly why all of them have unique names!

Big Mac with Extra Pickles

Big Mac is a hefty feline with a majestic coat of white adorned with gray stripes. Known for his love of food and cozy naps, Big Mac's size only adds to his charm. He's the cuddliest of the group. BTW HE COMES WITH EXTRA PICKLES!

Dave The Magical Cheese Wizard

Dave is a vibrant orange cat with a personality as bright as his coat. Always curious and magical, he seems to conjure magic wherever he goes. His enchanting presence makes him the heart of the undead, and he loves cheese. WINGARDIUM LEVIOSA!

Usman Abdul Jalil Sisha

Usman is a sleek white cat with soft gray fur patches that give him a distinguished and unique look. Calm and composed, he's the wise one among the group, always observing with a serene expression that feels almost philosophical. OKAY!

Schrödinger's Cat

Schrödinger is an ethereal beauty with a flawless, completely white coat. Mysterious and elegant, this cat leaves everyone guessing—whether she's in the box or out, she embodies an enigmatic allure that's impossible to ignore. WELL, SHE LOVES THE BOX THOUGH!

Muhammad Sumbul

Muhammad Sumbul is a striking orange cat who exudes confidence and charm. His spirited energy and playful antics make him a favorite among the group. HE WILL FIGHT FOR YOU!

1986 Ford F-150

Ford is a rugged gray cat with bold black stripes, giving him a tough and adventurous look. He’s the explorer of the bunch, always on the move and ready to take on any challenge with his cool, fearless attitude. VERY FAST!

MAA, The Cat's back!

It is a peculiar white cat with a unique, almost grandma-like appearance. Her quirky expressions and slightly eccentric demeanor make her endearing and unforgettable. SHE WILL NEVER LEAVE YOU ALONE!

Pablo Escobar

Pablo is a sleek and charming white cat with a sly twinkle in his eye. A master of sneaky adventures, he’s known for his bold escapades and mischievous tricks. HE CAN HOWEVER RUIN YOUR HOME!
